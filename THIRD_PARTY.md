## Inspiration (no code used)

Memo is a from-scratch clipboard history. It was inspired by other Mac clipboard managers, including Maccy (MIT licence, Copyright (c) Alex Rodionov, https://github.com/p0deje/Maccy). Memo contains no Maccy source code, so no Maccy licence notice is required; the credit is a courtesy. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Maccy or its author.
